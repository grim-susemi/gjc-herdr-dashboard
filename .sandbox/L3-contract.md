## Behaviour (AC3.1-AC3.3, features table row 7)
Per viewer (each role independent; one role's pointer or blur never touches the other role's prefs or ready file):
1. wheel: SGR button with bit 64 set. Wheel up (`button & 1 === 0`) scrolls -3 body lines, wheel down (`button & 1`) +3, clamped to [0, maxScroll]. Any wheel turns `follow` OFF, persists prefs (scroll, follow is NOT restored as OFF on reopen: AC1.3 already forces follow ON when reading prefs).
2. click (omo behaviour, copy it): left press (`(button & 3) === 0`, no wheel bit 64, no motion bit 32, not release). In the body rows only (`y-1 >= bodyStart && y-1 < bodyStart+bodyRows`), row = `y - 1 - bodyStart + scroll` is looked up in the frame ranges (Todo: `taskRanges`, Workers: `ranges`; first range with `row >= start && row < end`). A hit SELECTS that item and toggles its fold: Todo toggles `prefs.expanded` is NOT per-item in this repo, so for Todo a click only selects the task; Workers selects the card and toggles `prefs.expanded[id]` (same as Space/Enter). Every click (hit or miss, any row) turns `follow` OFF and starts a drag (`dragY = y`). Header/divider/footer/blank rows therefore only turn follow off.
3. drag (omo behaviour, copy it): a motion report with the left button (`(button & 3) === 0` and `button & 32`) while `dragY !== undefined` sets `scroll = max(0, scroll + dragY - y)` (clamped to maxScroll), then `dragY = y`; follow OFF. A report with `release === true` sets `dragY = undefined` and does nothing else. blur also clears `dragY`.
4. blur: when the pane loses focus (DEC 1004 `focus(false)` or `watchHerdrFocus` blur callback) and the viewer is not closing: follow returns ON, any transient detail view (`d` in Workers) closes, drag ends, selection-reveal is re-armed, and the frame is redrawn. Focus true does nothing. Prefs are saved when follow state changed. (AC3.2)
5. subscription failure (AC3.3): if `watchHerdrFocus` rejects or calls `failed(error)`, the viewer keeps running with DEC 1004 only and shows ONE notice line in the footer area (use the existing `notices` array; add a locale key `focusSubscriptionFailed` ("포커스 구독 실패: {error}" / English / zh-cn) via `t(language, key, params)` exactly like `prefsError`); it must not repeat the notice and must not throw. Call `watchHerdrFocus(options.pane, onBlur, onFailed, env)` only for interactive viewers, after the screen is entered; dispose it in `finish()`. If it resolves to a dispose function, call it on finish.
6. resize: already repaints; ensure the frame geometry used for click/drag mapping is the frame drawn after the resize.
7. All of the above must not write `slot.json` or `owner.json`, must not call Herdr except through `watchHerdrFocus`, and must not change `ready.json` semantics.
